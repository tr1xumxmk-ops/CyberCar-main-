Checks that save boards

  1. 1Main switch on the battery + wire. Keep it OFF while wiring.
  2. 2Set the buck converter to 5.00 V with a multimeter first. Adjustable bucks ship at random voltages – connect the ESP and servos only after.
  3. 3Remove the ENA / ENB jumpers on the L298N; speed comes from GPIO 11 / 12 (PWM). Leave its +5 V terminal empty.
  4. 4HC-SR04 ECHO is 5 V – the ESP32-S2 is 3.3 V only. Use the 1 kΩ + 2 kΩ divider shown.
  5. 5Servos from the 5 V buck, never from 3V3. Add a 1000 µF capacitor across the servo 5 V / GND rail (+ leg to 5 V).
  6. 6Battery sense uses GPIO 3 (ADC1). Measure the real divider ratio and put it in BATTERY_DIVIDER_RATIO. Max 8.4 V ÷ 3.2 = 2.6 V at the pin.

Parts list

  • LOLIN S2 Mini (ESP32-S2)
  • L298N dual motor driver + 2 DC gear motors (6 V class)
  • HC-SR04 + 1 kΩ and 2 kΩ resistors
  • 2 × SG90 / MG90S servo
  • 3 LEDs (R, G, B) + 3 × 220 Ω (or one common-cathode RGB LED)
  • Passive buzzer
  • 2S LiPo 7.4 V + a main switch
  • 5 V buck converter, ≥ 2 A
  • 22 kΩ + 10 kΩ resistors (battery divider)
  • 1000 µF / 10 V electrolytic capacitor
  • Wire, breadboard or perfboard, fuse (optional but wise)

Power notes

  • The L298N drops about 1.4–2 V, so motors see roughly 5.5–6.5 V from a 7.4 V pack.
  • Everything shares one GND: battery −, buck, L298N, S2 Mini, servos, sensor. A missing common ground is the #1 cause of random resets.
  • Motor start-up spikes can brown-out the ESP: keep motor wires away from the ECHO / servo signals.
  • First test: power with the motors unplugged from the L298N, join the car's WiFi and check distance + battery on the dashboard.
  • Avoid GPIO 0 (BOOT) and GPIO 15 (on-board LED).

ملخص بالعربي

  • الباك كونفرتر لازم يتضبط على 5.00 فولت بالمالتي ميتر قبل ما توصّل الـESP والسيرفو.
  • كل الـGND مشترك: البطارية، الباك، L298N، الـESP، السيرفو والحساس.
  • سلك ECHO يطلع 5V، لازم مقسّم جهد (1kΩ + 2kΩ) حتى ما يتحرق الـESP.
  • شيل جمبرات ENA / ENB من الـL298N.
  • السيرفو من الباك 5V، مو من 3V3، وحط كابستر 1000µF.
  • إذا موتر يدور بالعكس، بدّل سلكين المخرج مالته.
